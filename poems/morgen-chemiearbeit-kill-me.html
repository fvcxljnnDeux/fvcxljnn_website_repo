Morgen Chemiearbeit, kill me!

10/03/2023

I miss that moment when I'd failed
Sat frail within my couch's corner
Cried and waved a life derailed
Ephemeral predictive dooms
To then be free of sorrows bound
By artificial place and ground
That posed the source of all my glooms
And veiled my sight off real delight
