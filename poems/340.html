Imagine in the Water

[340] 17/07/2026

In every drop of rain imagine
The restless roaring sea –
So when you catch one with your tongue
You taste the stark infinity.

In every tear drop just imagine
Roots of ancient glee –
Now go and find yourself among
Those wandering eternity.
