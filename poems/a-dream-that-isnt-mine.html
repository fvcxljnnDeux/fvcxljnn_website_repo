A Dream That Isn't Mine

??/??/2023

It's too early to tell
If still early or hell –
But I'm dumped at the place,
Being shrunk and erased,
My mind numbed and liaised –

And after the swimming,
And after the swell –
No one is winning,
There's no new beginning –
I'm stuck with the waves.
