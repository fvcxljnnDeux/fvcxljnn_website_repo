Tortured Artist

[319] 26/06/2026

I took it up with my bottle –
And asked it for advice –
It served me up a quatrain,
And warned to pay the price;
I took four sips and coughed at it,
It smiled and offered more –
Eight sips, it said, an octet cost –
I wonder now what for...
