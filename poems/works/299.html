A Crooked Ring

[299] 06/06/2026

A crooked ring sits on my finger,
Its corner points towards me;
The waves are lovely, dark and deep,
And so is the hole in the ring –
I may not fall through it,
But I also can't hold onto it;
A crooked ring sits on my finger.
