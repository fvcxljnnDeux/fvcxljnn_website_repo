Time knocked on my door –

11/08/2025

Time knocked on my door – and I killed it.
Now the sun just comes and goes,
And the moon consoles me in my whiplash.
My eyes, having wandered through endless
Days of the awoken world,
Yearn for seeing no more.
But rest is merely a mirage –
I live in eternal daybreak.
