Mottowoche - Tag 2

21/03/2023

Half past sober
In a corner
Watching people
Dance and cheer

Eyes are heavy
Sinking deeper
People nodding
Fake a smile

Like they're taunting
All my failures
Singing ABBA
Need to piss

Hate my habits
Know nobody
Just spectating
Want to die
