My Mind Can't Skip a Rope

[107] 26/11/2025

I am a rigid thinker,
My mind can't skip a rope;
I think in clear intentions,
I'm lacking further scope.

When people talk to me
I satisfy their needs,
But I don't know what else to say –
The conversation breathes...
