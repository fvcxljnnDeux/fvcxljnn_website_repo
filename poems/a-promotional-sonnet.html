A Promotional Sonnet

07/02/2023

Thou mayest think us thespians pedestrian
But that shan't ground our dreaming dove
'Cause our play has all, from loss to lesbians
So come and watch "Shakespeare in Laugh"

On February 21st, a day of Tues
When 19:30 strike the clocks
Be present when we put the Forum to good use
And witness our acting chops

With pupils paying twice the worth of but a buck
And adults merely three bucks more
We aim to fight your apathy and make you shrug
And say: "Well, this looks not like such a bore."

What's left to do on this world's stage is let each player play his part
For us that is quite literal, but you just open purse and heart
