I Befriended Illness

[166] 24/01/2026

I befriended illness –
Instead of taking desperate measures
I let it lay around me
Its arms of idle leisure

But our time is almost over
Soon I'll breathe past the blockades again
I'll concentrate again on work
Dear friend, it was a pleasure
