P.J.

[343] 20/07/2026

Upon his recognition
Of my being
Who I was,
I reached out
To his memory –
And offered him my hand.
He read the signs –
Confused –
Outgrown the seasons –
Lost –
And now within him
Slept
The son of time
And its demand.
