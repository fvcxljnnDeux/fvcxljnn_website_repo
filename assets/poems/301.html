Crunch Time

We're near the end of the semester –
I've got a hunch the time says crunch –
But even though this ain't my first rodeo,
This could be the worst one of the bunch;

Tomorrow will bring a fight for new hope –
It'll be a two-front war –
And starting next week it'll be torture each day –
I have to remember what I'm doing this for.
