The Deaths of Two Days

Watch the day clothed in good intentions
Fade into lazy nakedness
It is by your doing that it's freezing now
Freezing to its death

Tomorrow there will be a new companion
Another stretch of time for you to knead
And it too will crumble down and die
But maybe its death you will have given meaning?
